# Privacy Policy (Draft)

StateWatch processes HealthKit data on-device for wellness readiness insights.

## Data handling (MVP)
- HealthKit data remains local on device
- No upload of HealthKit data to external servers
- No use of HealthKit data for advertising, marketing, or data mining

## User controls
- Users can manage Health permissions in system settings
- Users can uninstall the app to remove local app data

## Medical disclaimer
StateWatch is for general wellness guidance and is not a medical diagnosis tool.
