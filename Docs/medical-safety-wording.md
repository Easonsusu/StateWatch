# Medical Safety Wording

## Approved positioning

- "Daily wellness reflection"
- "Recovery signal"
- "Sleep trend"
- "Stress/fatigue context"
- "Activity load"
- "May suggest"
- "Consider taking it easier"
- "Based on available local data"

## Avoid

- "Detects illness"
- "Diagnoses disease"
- "Predicts sickness"
- "Medical-grade"
- "Treatment recommendation"
- "Prevents disease"
- "You are sick"
- "Abnormal heart condition"

## Disclaimer draft

StateWatch provides wellness reflections based on available Apple Health and check-in data. It is not a medical device and does not provide medical diagnosis, treatment, or disease detection. If you have medical concerns, consult a qualified healthcare professional.

## Suggestion tone

Suggestions should be gentle and optional. Good examples:

- "Consider a lighter activity day if that matches how you feel."
- "A consistent bedtime tonight may support recovery."
- "Your check-in can help add context to today's signals."
